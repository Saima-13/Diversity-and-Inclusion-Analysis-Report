
# 🌍 Diversity & Inclusion Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Diversity & Inclusion Analysis Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyze workforce diversity, gender representation, employee turnover, hiring patterns, and promotion trends. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of employee demographics and career progression, helping organizations understand diversity across functions, departments, age groups, nationalities, regions, and job levels.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objective of this project is to analyze workforce diversity and inclusion across different organizational dimensions. The dashboard aims to evaluate gender representation, hiring and turnover trends, employee promotions, performance ratings, nationality and regional diversity, and representation across departments and job levels. It also provides interactive filtering options to help users explore diversity patterns and identify potential areas for improving workplace inclusion and equal career opportunities.

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard helps identify several important workforce trends:

* The workforce consists of **500 employees**, with male employees representing 59% and female employees representing 41%.
* Female hiring represents **51.5%**, compared with 48.5% male hiring.
* The overall turnover rate is **10.64%**.
* Male and female average performance ratings are very similar, at **2.41 and 2.42** respectively.
* Employee promotion rates can be compared across FY2020 and FY2021.
* Female promotion trends provide an opportunity to evaluate gender representation in career advancement.
* Gender representation varies across functions, departments, age groups, and job levels.
* Nationality and regional analysis provides insights into the geographic diversity of the workforce.
